# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant] in full.

In short: participate respectfully. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning or exclusionary
behaviour are not welcome here, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else the project
is represented.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take fair corrective action in
response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, up to and including a permanent ban.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory][advisory] or emailing the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
All complaints will be reviewed promptly and in confidence. Maintainers must recuse themselves from
enforcement concerning incidents they are involved in.

The [full text][covenant] governs, including its enforcement guidelines and the ladder of
consequences (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
[advisory]: https://github.com/mohamedaboelmagd/typeorm-resilient-transactional/security/advisories/new
